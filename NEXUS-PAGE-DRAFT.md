## Title

Second Crucible Unlock and Encounter Fixes (Build 22928553)

## Short description

Temporarily unlocks the second Crucible and repairs its offering sequence, room progression, and Warrick phase-two target on the supported Steam build.

## Requirements

- Legitimate Steam copy of No Rest for the Wicked, public Build 22928553
- Compatible MelonLoader 0.7.x

## What has been tested

One complete nine-floor run on 2026-10-05 matched the seven configured Boss encounters, checkpoint, and reward room. The second-bowl cut, blood drop, and floor blood route were visually checked in an earlier run. Other game builds and repeated reload scenarios are unverified.

## Installation and removal

Use the steps in `INSTALL.md`. Download MelonLoader from its official source; it is not bundled. Back up saves first. Close the game before installing, updating, or removing the DLL.
